Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept every task?

No. Every task is reviewed first. Only tasks that can be completed safely and properly are accepted.

Do I need to send photos?

Yes. Photos help determine the tools, time, difficulty, and correct price.

Do you bring tools?

Basic tools may be brought depending on the task. Customers will be told beforehand if special tools or supplies are needed.

Do you provide licensed repairs?

No. Electrical, gas, structural, major plumbing, roofing, HVAC, and other licensed work are not offered.

Is this service only for older women?

No. The service is designed for women of different ages, including seniors, disabled women, women living alone, and anyone who prefers working with another woman.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo, and Cash App are all accepted. Payment is due at the end of the visit, once the work is done and you're happy with it.
